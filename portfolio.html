I think in two directions at once — one eye on the data, one on the people it's meant to serve.

I'm currently in my second year of a Computer Science degree at Redeemer's University, building toward roles in data science and machine learning. Most of my learning happens outside the lecture hall: I've completed data science internships at CodeAlpha and DecodeLabs, I'm recently completed Udacity's AWS AI/ML Nanodegree, and I recently took on a role as an AWS Student Builder Group Leader.

But I'm not only a person who builds models, I'm a person who builds rooms. I volunteered with TEDxRUN, and as part of my department's PR Committee I co-organised Departmental Week for 1,000+ students — covering OOTDs, interviewing people, and shooting content for the department. It taught me that the best technical work still has to be explained to, and championed by, other people. That's the thread running through everything below: rigorous where it needs to be rigorous, human where it needs to be human.
